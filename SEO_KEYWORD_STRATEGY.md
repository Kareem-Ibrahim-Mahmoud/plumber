# SEO Keyword Strategy — Sahhi Kuwait

**Note on methodology:** No professional keyword research tool or search-volume data is available in this environment. All opportunities below are **qualitative hypotheses** derived from the website's actual content and business model; search volumes, difficulty scores, and ranking promises are intentionally NOT included.

---

## 1. Business and audience understanding

- **What:** 24-hour emergency plumber service in Kuwait (فني صحي بالكويت).
- **Audience:** Residents and property managers in Kuwait needing urgent plumbing help (blocked drains/sewers, pump/filter installation, sanitaryware repairs), primarily mobile users searching in Arabic.
- **Location:** Kuwait — all governorates (Hawally, Salmiya, Ahmadi, Kuwait City, Mishref + others).
- **Language:** Arabic (Kuwaiti/Gulf terminology), RTL layout.
- **Conversion:** Phone call +96566115976, WhatsApp message, contact form.

## 3. Keyword mapping — homepage (`/`)

Only one public URL exists, so the homepage carries all intents; no cannibalization risk across pages.

- **Primary target phrase:** فني صحي بالكويت / سباكة وتسليك مجاري بدون تكسير
- **Secondary phrases:** تركيب مضخات المياه الكويت، تركيب فلاتر المياه، تسليك البواليع، رداد المجاري، صحي 24 ساعة
- **Search intent:** Emergency commercial (immediate need) + informational (how to fix blocked drains)
- **Proposed title (current):** فني صحي بالكويت 24 ساعة | سباكة وتسليك مجاري بدون تكسير
- **Proposed H1 (current):** فني صحي بالكويت — [24 ساعة] — نصلك أينما كنت
- **Content gaps:** No dedicated service pages (each service is a card on the homepage); no per-area pages (intent exists: "صحي حولي" etc., but creating thin doorway pages for every area is prohibited by quality constraints — areas are covered on the homepage Areas section).
- **Related internal links:** Services section → CTA call; Areas section → call CTA; Articles section → informational content.
- **Conversion goal:** Phone call or WhatsApp message.

---

## 4. Content guidance

- Do NOT create dozens of near-identical area/service pages (doorway pages).
- Do NOT fabricate statistics, reviews, awards, or case studies.
- Keep Arabic copy natural (Gulf/Kuwaiti terminology); avoid literal machine-translated English phrasing.
- Existing H1/H2 structure: one H1 (hero), H2 per section (services, work, areas, articles, contact, why us, process) — logical hierarchy preserved.

---

## 5. Assumptions

- Searchers use Arabic, predominantly on mobile, with high urgency (24-hour emergency framing matches intent).
- "بدون تكسير" (without demolition) is a genuine local differentiator mentioned throughout the site copy and service cards.
